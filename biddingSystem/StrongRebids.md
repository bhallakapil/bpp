# Strong Re-bids and Jump Rebids in Contract Bridge

> **Scope:** Detailed structural analysis of Opener's strong re-bids after a 1-level response (`1x – 1y`), covering Reverses, Jump Shifts, Jump Rebids, `1x – 1y – 2NT`, Double Jumps / High-Level Rebids, with inline responder follow-ups, convention names, and **Ingberman 2NT**.  
> **Core Principle:** Hand construction is determined by positive distribution and playing strength. Unbalanced shapes and distributional power dictate these forward-going re-bids.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy
When opener opens 1-of-a-suit and responder replies at the 1-level (`1♣/♦/♥ – 1♥/♠`), responder has shown 6+ HCP and an unpassed hand. Opener's rebid defines the partnership's auction trajectory. Hands with extreme distribution, extra values, or offensive trick-taking potential utilize advanced forcing tools, jump rebids, and strong suit-showing jumps to explore game and slam based on constructive hand geometry.

---

## 2. The Reverse (`1x – 1y – 2z`)
- **Definition:** Bidding a second suit at the 2-level that is *higher-ranking* than the opening suit (e.g., `1♦ – 1♠ – 2♥`).
- **Hand Construction:** Built on a 5-4, 6-4, or 5-5 unbalanced distribution with 17+ HCP (or equivalent playing strength of $\le 4.5$ losers). The first suit is longer or equal in length to the second suit, requiring extra values to rebid at the 2-level in a higher-ranking suit.
- **Forcing Status:** 100% Forcing for one round. All subsequent bids (except 2NT or opening suit preference) are forcing at the 3-level.
- **Responder Inline Follow-Up Actions & Conventions (including Ingberman):**
  - **Cheapest Return to Opener's 1st Suit (`2x`) — Pass-or-Correct / Signoff:** Weak (6–9 HCP), showing preference for the opening suit. Opener passes.
  - **2NT Rebid by Responder — The Ingberman Convention:** Artificial forcing inquiry over a reverse. When responder bids 2NT after opener's reverse, it asks opener to clarify their strength/shape (minimum vs maximum) without committing to game. Opener bids their cheapest natural rebid or describes their hand, allowing responder to sign off at the 3-level in the first suit or invite/drive to game safely.
  - **Raise of Opener's 2nd Suit (`3z`) — Invitational to Game:** Shows 10–11 HCP and 3+ card support. Opener passes with minimum or bids game with max.
  - **New Suit at 2-level / 3-level — Lebensohl / Game Forcing:** Establishes 1GF. Bidding 4th suit or raising (utilizing **Lebensohl** principles over competitive or reverse structures) implies slam exploration and control showing.

## 3. The Jump Shift (`1x – 1y – 3z`)
- **Definition:** Opener jumps in a new suit at the 3-level (e.g., `1♦ – 1♥ – 3♣`).
- **Hand Construction:** Built on powerful offensive distribution (5-5 or strong 6-4 shape) with 17+ HCP and high control content (Aces/Kings). The hand is geared toward trick-taking via distribution and outside honors rather than slow positional investigation.
- **Forcing Status:** Game Forcing (1GF). Establishes an immediate game force and explores slam potential.
- **Responder Inline Follow-Up Actions & Conventions:**
  - **Raise of Jump Shift Suit (`4z`) — To Play / Natural Fit:** Confirms fit and sign-off in game.
  - **Cuebid of Opponent's / Other Suit / 4NT — Slam Exploration / RKCB:** Initiates cue-bidding sequence or asks for keycards (**Roman Key Card Blackwood**).

---

## 4. The Jump Rebid (`1x – 1y – 3x`)
- **Definition:** Opener jumps in their own opening suit (e.g., `1♥ – 1♠ – 3♥`).
- **Hand Construction:** Built on a robust 6+ card suit with 16–18 HCP and a semi-solid or solid suit structure. Lacks support for responder's major but possesses enough playing strength to insist on game opposite a normal response.
- **Forcing Status:** Invitational to game (non-forcing, but highly encouraging).
- **Responder Inline Follow-Up Actions:**
  - **Pass — Signoff:** Responder has minimum values (6–8 HCP) and no fit for opener's suit.
  - **Raise to 4-level (`4x`) — To Play:** Natural sign-off in game based on combined suit length/fit.

---

## 5. The 2NT Rebid (`1x – 1y – 2NT`)
- **Definition:** Opener rebids 2NT directly after a 1-level response (e.g., `1♣ – 1♥ – 2NT`).
- **Hand Construction:** Built on a heavy balanced structure (typically 18–19 HCP, such as 4333, 4432, or 5332 without a major fit).
- **Conventions Active:** **New Minor Forcing (NMF) / XYZ / Checkback Stayman**.
- **Responder Inline Follow-Up Actions & Conventions:**
  - **New Minor at 3-level (`3♣` / `3♦`) — New Minor Forcing (NMF) / XYZ:** Artificial inquiry asking opener to show 3-card support for responder's major or stopped unbid suits.
  - **Direct 3NT / 4M — To Play / Signoff:** Natural sign-off in game with balanced values or major fit.

## 6. Double Jumps and High-Level Rebids

### A. Jump Raise of Responder's Major (`1m – 1M – 3M`)
- **Definition:** Opener jumps directly to 3 of responder's major (e.g., `1♦ – 1♠ – 3♠` or `1♣ – 1♥ – 3♥`).
- **Hand Construction:** Built on 16–18 support points with 4-card support (or very robust 3-card support). The hand relies on trump control and distributional values to drive toward game.
- **Responder Inline Follow-Up Actions:**
  - **4M — Acceptance:** Natural sign-off in game opposite invitational values (16–18 support points).
  - **New Suit / Cuebid — Slam Try:** Responder has extra values (11+ support points) and explores slam via cue-bids.

### B. Strong Suit-Showing Jump (`1m – 1M – 4m`)
- **Definition:** Opener jumps to 4 of their minor after responder's 1-level major response (e.g., `1♣ – 1♥ – 4♣` or `1♦ – 1♠ – 4♦`).
- **Hand Construction:** Built on a long, robust, self-sufficient minor suit (6+ cards with 2 of top 3 honors) alongside extra offensive values, establishing a powerful minor suit playing strength.

### C. Natural Game-Force / Choice of Games (`1m – 1x – 3NT`)
- **Definition:** Opener jumps directly to 3NT after a 1-level response (e.g., `1♣ – 1♦ – 3NT` or `1♦ – 1♠ – 3NT`).
- **Hand Construction:** Built on a heavy balanced 18–19 HCP structure with rounded stoppers, or a solid running minor suit where 3NT provides the optimal game contract.

### D. Fast Arrival Game Sign-off (`1M – 1x – 4M`)
- **Definition:** Opener jumps directly to game in responder's major (e.g., `1♥ – 1♠ – 4♥` or `1♠ – 1NT – 4♠`).
- **Hand Construction:** Built on minimum opening values (12–15 HCP) with an agreed major fit, utilizing **Fast Arrival** to conclude the auction at game without slam aspirations.
