# ACBL Alert Procedure - Executive Summary & Grouped Reference

**Effective Date:** February 16, 2026

---

## Grouped Quick Reference & Complete Example Index

This document categorizes every rule, requirement, exception, and example from [`laws/Alert-Procedures.pdf`](laws/Alert-Procedures.pdf) into three primary operational groups: **Announceable**, **Alertable**, and **Non-Alertable (Exceptions)**.

---

### 1. ANNOUNCEABLE CALLS & AGREEMENTS
*Announcements describe the meaning of partner's call using a word or short phrase and must be made immediately along with a visual alert.*

* **Minor Suit Openings (Non-Forcing):** Minimum length of any Non-Forcing minor suit Opening that could be fewer than 3 cards (Form: “Could be X”).
  * *Examples:*
    * A Quasi-Natural 1C Opening Bid that includes all balanced hands outside the 1NT Opening range → Announce **“Could be two.”**
    * A Quasi-Natural 1C Opening Bid that includes hands with singleton A, K, or Q → Announce **“Could be one.”**
    * A Non-Forcing 1D Opening that might not contain any diamonds → Announce **“Could be zero.”**
    * *Note:* A Forcing 1C Opening showing 16+ HCP that might not contain any clubs → **“Alert.”**

* **Natural 1NT Openings:** The HCP Range of any Natural 1NT Opening.
  * *Examples:*
    * A 1NT Opening Bid that shows 15-17 HCP → Announce **“Fifteen to Seventeen.”**
    * A 1NT Opening Bid that generally shows 12-14 HCP, but is frequently opened with 11 HCP → Announce **“Good Eleven to Fourteen.”**

* **Suit-Showing Bids, Doubles, and Redoubles:** Any Artificial bid, double, or redouble that primarily shows Length in one specific suit. Use the name of the suit being shown (does not apply to suits where you/partner have already shown length).
  * *Examples:*
    * After a 1NT opening, a 2D bid showing hearts → Announce **“Hearts.”**
    * After a 1C opening and a Takeout Double, a 1H bid showing spades → Announce **“Spades.”**
    * After a 1D opening and a Takeout Double, a redouble showing hearts → Announce **“Hearts.”**
    * After a 1D opening and a Takeout Double, a redouble showing spades → Announce **“Spades.”**
    * As Advancer in auction `(1C) 1S (P)`, a 2D bid showing hearts → Announce **“Hearts.”**
    * After a Very Strong 2C Opening, a 2D bid showing hearts → Announce **“Hearts.”**
    * A 4D Opening Bid showing spades → Announce **“Spades.”**
    * After a Preemptive 2D Opening, a 2NT response showing spades → Announce **“Spades.”**
    * After a 1D Opening and a 1H Direct Overcall, a Double showing 4 or more spades → Announce **“Spades.”**
    * After a 1NT Opening and a 2H Direct Overcall, a Double showing 5 or more spades → Announce **“Spades.”**

* **1NT Responses to 1-Level Major Openings:** After a 1-level major suit Opening in first or second seat, a 1NT response that is Forcing or Semi-Forcing. Also announce “could have 4 spades” in the unopposed auction `1H - 1NT` if you routinely bypass a 4-card spade suit.
  * *Examples:*
    * After a first seat 1H opening, a 1NT Response that is Semi-Forcing → Announce **“Semi-Forcing.”**
    * After a first seat 1H Opening, a 1NT Response that is Forcing and also routinely has 4 spades because the pair plays Flannery → Announce **“Forcing, could have 4 spades.”**

### 2. ALERTABLE CALLS & AGREEMENTS
*General Rules: Default rule for Natural Bids & Passes is **do not alert** (exceptions in 2A/2B). Default rule for Artificial Bids is **alert** (exceptions in 3B).*

#### A. Natural Bids to Alert
* **Opening Bids:** Natural 2C opening; 2/3-level suit opening containing 12+ HCP (not 4th seat); Natural preemptive bid with a second way to show the same suit at the same level; Natural opening where a later suit bid is routinely longer; Natural opening NT always based on long suit. (*Examples:* Weak 2C: **“Alert.”**; 10-15 HCP 6+ clubs 2C: **“Alert.”**; Strong 2C: **“Alert.”**; 4H Natural Preempt when 4D is stronger: **“Alert.”**; 3D Natural Preempt when 2S is weaker: **“Alert.”**; 3NT opening on running minor + outside stoppers: **“Alert.”**)
* **Direct Overcalls:** Natural 1NT direct overcall < 14 HCP; Natural 2-level cuebid; Natural jump overcall showing average+ strength; Natural 2NT showing a strong hand; Natural direct overcall in same suit at 2+ level. (*Examples:* Natural 2C overcall over 1C: **“Alert.”**; 3H overcall showing 11-15 HCP and 6-card heart suit over 1S: **“Alert.”**)
* **Responses & Rebids:** Non-forcing new suit response at cheapest level (unpassed hand); 1-level non-forcing new suit response over double; Forcing single raise of natural 1-level opening; Double raise not showing invitational values; Non-forcing new suit/2NT response below game after preempt; Below-game raise after preempt (invitational/forcing); 1NT rebid / major raise containing 16+ HCP (unless opener showed strong hand); Rebid showing longer suit than opened; Rebid showing 5-card major after 2C/3C asking over NT sequence/overcall; Natural bid showing length/values in another suit. (*Examples:* Non-forcing 1H response over 1C: **“Alert.”**; Non-forcing 2H response over 1C and 1S overcall: **“Alert.”**; Non-forcing 1S response over 1H and Takeout Double: **“Alert.”**; Preemptive or Forcing 3S response over natural 1S opening and pass: **“Alert.”**; Non-forcing 2NT response over natural 2D preempt: **“Alert.”**; 2H opening showing hearts and minor: **“Alert.”**; Opener's 2S rebid showing 4 spades and club stopper in `1NT (P) 2C (X)`: **“Alert.”**; 3H response showing both majors over 1S opening and 2C overcall: **“Alert.”**)

#### B. Passes to Alert
* Forcing pass when hand passing has not previously shown strength (unless partner showed Very Strong). (*Example:* Pass that is Forcing after 1NT opening, penalty double, and 2H response: **“Alert.”**)
* Pass showing hand is not minimum strength in context of current auction. (*Example:* Pass showing 4+ HCP after Very Strong 2C opening and 2H overcall: **“Alert.”**)
* Pass showing Length or strength in a suit neither shown by partner nor opponents. (*Example:* Pass asking overcaller to bid 2S with diamonds and 3C with clubs after `1H (2H) X`: **“Alert.”**)

#### C. Artificial Bids, Doubles, and Redoubles to Alert
* **Artificial Bids (Default Alert):** All artificial bids *unless listed as exceptions in Section 3B*, plus any Quasi-Natural bid that is not Announced. (*Examples:* 2NT response asking about shape after 2D opening showing 3-suited hand: **“Alert.”**; 3C game try asking for help in club suit after 1H opening and 2H response: **“Alert.”**; 2S bid showing clubs or diamonds after 1NT opening: **“Alert.”**; 3D bid showing hearts or raise to 3S wanting heart lead as Responder in `2S (X)`: **“Alert.”**; 1S bid showing balanced hand after 1D opening and Takeout Double: **“Alert.”**; 3H bid showing game force with 6+ spades after unopposed `1NT - 2H - 2S` transfer: **“Alert.”**; 3D rebid showing no 5-card major but showing 4-card major after 2NT opening and 3C response asking majors: **“Alert.”**)
* **Doubles (Default Alert):** Double of an artificial bid asking for specific lead / not to lead. Doubles below 3NT *unless listed as exceptions in Section 3C*. (*Examples:* Double showing fewer than 4 spades after 1D opening and 1H overcall: **“Alert.”**; Takeout Double after Preemptive 2D opening and 2H overcall: **“Alert.”**; Takeout Double after 1H opening and 1NT overcall: **“Alert.”**)
* **Opener’s Rebid and Later:** **All doubles by both sides are Alertable.**
* **Redoubles (Default Alert):** All redoubles *unless listed as exceptions in Section 3D*.
* **Delayed Alerts:** Control bids must be Alerted at the end of the auction even if below 3NT. Bids 3NT or higher at or beyond opener's rebid are delayed until end of auction. (*Examples:* In `1H - 2NT - 3D - 4S - 5C - 6H`, Alert 2NT and 3D immediately, explain 4S and 5C at end of auction. In `1S - 2C - 2H - 3H - 3S - 4C - 4H`, explain 3S and 4C control bids at end of auction.)

### 3. NON-ALERTABLE / NON-ANNOUNCEABLE CALLS & AGREEMENTS (EXCEPTIONS)

#### A. Natural Bids (Default: No Alert)
* Standard natural bids, natural overcalls, and natural responses not meeting any alert exception criteria. (*Examples:* Natural 2D overcall over Precision 1D showing 2+ diamonds: **No Alert or Announcement required.**; Natural 3C overcall over Natural 1C: **No Alert or Announcement required.**; Non-forcing or Forcing 2C response over 1H opening and Takeout Double: **No Alert or Announcement required.**; Preemptive or Forcing 3S response over natural 1S opening and Takeout Double: **No Alert or Announcement required.**; 2H direct overcall showing spades and unspecified minor over natural 1H opening: **Do not Alert or Announce.**)

#### B. Artificial Bids (Do NOT Alert - Exceptions)
* **Opening Bids:** A Very Strong Artificial 2C Opening Bid.
* **Direct Overcalls:**
  * 2-level Cuebid showing length in both majors over a Natural 1-level minor suit bid. (*Example:* 2D overcall showing both majors over Natural 1D: **Do not Alert or Announce.**)
  * 2-level Cuebid showing length in unbid major and unspecified minor over a Natural 1-level major suit bid. (*Example:* 2H overcall showing spades and unspecified minor over Natural 1H: **Do not Alert or Announce.**)
  * Any Cuebid after a Quasi-Natural Opening Bid. (*Examples:* 2D overcall showing both majors or spades and clubs over Precision 1D showing 2+ diamonds: **Do not Alert or Announce.**)
  * Any bid in the same suit or shown suit after an Artificial Opening Bid.
  * 2NT Overcall showing lowest two unbid suits after a Natural 1-level Opening Bid.
  * 2NT Overcall showing two suits (known or unknown) after a Quasi-Natural or Artificial Opening Bid.
  * Any bid in the same suit or shown suit after a Natural Opening Bid at the 2-level or higher.
  * Any Cuebid that is a jump. (*Examples:* 3H bid showing spades and minor or asking stopper for NT over 2H Preempt: **Do not Alert or Announce.**)
* **Responses to Opening Bids & Overcalls:**
  * Forcing 2NT response to a 2-level Preempt regardless of meaning. (*Examples:* 2NT response asking outside ace/king or strength after Preemptive 2D opening: **Do not Alert or Announce.**)
  * 2C over 1NT or 3C over 2NT asking about major suit holdings (unless follow-up reveals no major). (*Examples:* 2C asking major length in unopposed `1C - 1D - 1NT` where 1C was strong, 1D was 0-7 HCP, 1NT was 17-19 bal: **Do not Alert or Announce.**; 3C asking major length in `2C - 2D - 2H - 2S - 2NT` where 2C was very strong, 2D waiting, 2H hearts/bal, 2S automatic, 2NT 24+ bal: **Do not Alert or Announce.**)
  * 2D response to a Strong (or Very Strong) Artificial 2C Opening Bid. (*Examples:* 2D response showing game force, control count 0-1, or waiting after Strong 2C opening: **Do not Alert or Announce.**; 2H response showing game force with hearts after Strong 2C opening: **Do not Alert or Announce.**)
* **Other Artificial Bids:**
  * 4C bid asking for aces or keycards directly over a No Trump bid or in an auction starting with an Opening No Trump Sequence.
  * 4NT bid asking for aces or keycards.
  * Cuebid by either side showing a raise, average+ strength, or either after an Opening Suit Bid and Overcall.
  * Any bid in same suit or shown suit after an Opening No Trump Bid and Overcall.
  * Any non-opening bid in the same suit as opening/response or in a shown suit after an Opening Bid and Response.
  * Any suit bid showing strength or 3+ cards in the suit after an Opening Bid and Response. (*Examples:* 3H game try showing 3+ card heart suit after 1H opening and 2H response: **Do not alert or announce.**; 2S bid showing spade stopper after 1D opening and 2D response: **Do not alert or announce.**)
  * Any No Trump Overcall at the 4-level or higher.
  * 2D response denying 4-card major after 2C major asking bid, and 3D response denying 4-card major after 3C major asking bid. (*Example:* 3D rebid showing no 4-card major after 2NT opening and 3C response asking majors: **Do not alert or announce.**)
  * 2NT Overcall showing two suits (known or unknown) after a 1-level Opening Bid and Response. (*Examples:* 2NT overcall showing diamonds and spades or minors after Natural 1C opening and 1H response: **Do not alert or announce.**)

#### C. Doubles (Do NOT Alert - Exceptions)
* **After Opening Bid:** Takeout double of natural/quasi-natural suit opening at any level. Penalty/card-showing double of NT opening. Any double after artificial opening.
* **After Opening Bid & Overcall:** Negative double by responder after 1-level suit opening and natural overcall (*Example:* Double showing exactly 4 spades after 1D opening and 1H direct overcall: **Do not Alert or Announce.**). Any double by responder after artificial overcall. Penalty double by responder after 2+-level suit opening and natural overcall (*Example:* Penalty double after Preemptive 2D opening and 2H overcall: **Do not Alert or Announce.**). Penalty double after natural 1NT/2NT overcall (and any double after artificial NT/2NT overcall). Negative, penalty, and card-showing doubles after NT opening and 2/3-level overcall (*Examples:* Takeout or penalty double after 1NT opening and 2H overcall: **Do not Alert or Announce.**).
* **After Opening Bid & Response:** Takeout and penalty doubles after natural/quasi-natural opening and response sequences. Any double if opening or response is artificial. Any double after natural opening, natural overcall, and natural 1NT response.
* **At Opener’s Rebid and Later:** Double by Opener showing 3 hearts or penalty double in auction `1C (P) 1H (1S)`: **Do not Alert or Announce.**

#### D. Redoubles (Do NOT Alert - Exceptions)
* Redouble showing general strength suggesting playing current contract or penalizing others. (*Example:* Redouble suggesting playing 3NT in `(2H) X (P) 3NT; (P) P (X)`: **Do not Alert or Announce.**)
* Redouble showing specific number of cards or strength in partner’s suit. (*Examples:* Redouble showing 3 hearts in `1D (P) 1H (X)`: **Do not Alert or Announce.**; Redouble showing Ace or King of spades in `(1D) 1S (X)`: **Do not Alert or Announce.**)
* Redouble in balancing seat or after balancing double suggesting doubt or playing different contract. (*Example:* Redouble suggesting playing different contract in `(2H) X (P) 3NT; (P) P (X)`: **Do not Alert or Announce.**)
* Redouble showing control in doubled suit.
