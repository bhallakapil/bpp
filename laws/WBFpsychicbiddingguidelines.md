# WBF Psychic Bidding Guidelines

**Document:** `laws/WBFpsychicbiddingguidelines.pdf`  
**Status:** Adopted July 1995, revised August 2002, revised August 2022  
**Published:** March 2023 by the Systems Committee of the World Bridge Federation

## Overview

These guidelines clarify the distinction between psychic calls and partnership understandings in the context of the Laws of Duplicate Bridge 2017.

## Key Principles

1.  **Psychic Call Definition**: A psychic call is permitted provided it is *not* based on a partnership understanding.
2.  **Partnership Understanding**: An understanding exists when the frequency of occurrence is sufficient for the partner to take the possibility into account. Such understandings must be disclosed beforehand.
3.  **Prohibitions**:
    *   Explicit agreements that psychic calls are expected are prohibited.
    *   Systemic protection for psychic calls is prohibited.
4.  **Disclosure Requirements**:
    *   Understandings where gross violations can be anticipated must be disclosed on the system card.
    *   These must be listed among conventions calling for special defense.
    *   Supplementary sheets must provide full details of situations where violations may occur.
5.  **Random Psychics**: Random psychic calls are still allowed as long as they cannot be anticipated by the partner any more than by the opponents.
6.  **Card Play**: Similar principles apply to psychic actions in a defender's play of the cards (deviations from documented card-play agreements).
