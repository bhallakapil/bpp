# Simulation 1 million hands

## 1NT Opening
- **HCP**: 15-17 HCP
- **Shape**: 4333, 4432, 5332, 6322 with 6-card minors.

### LHO
Evaluate with Mel’s rule of 8 to bid:
- **DBL**: 4-card major and longer minor
- **2C**: D or M/m
- **2D**: Majors
- **2H, 2S**: Natural
- **2N**: Both minors

### Partner
- 0-7 HCP Passes without:
  - 5+ Card Major (transfer)
  - 6+ Card Minor (transfer)

### RHO
- Use Mel’s Rule of 2 or Pass
- Same system for bidding

## Opening bidder uses Super Accepts

Partner bids game over 2NT or change of suit (without facing doubletons) with 7 dummy points or 2 ½ cover cards.

- Evaluate each result with Double Dummy play.
- Accumulate the average result for all 4 vulnerabilities.

Repeat for Partner Passing with a 5-card major.

### Repeat with Standard Opponent

#### LHO
- Dbl with 15+ or Bids 2 of suit with 6-card suit.
- RHO passes double without 6-card suit.

#### RHO
- Passes without 12+ and 6-card suit.

---

## Cover Cards
- **1 cover card**: A, K, singleton
- **½ cover card**: Q or doubleton
- **-½ cover cards**: 
  - Number of (Aces - Number of Q) if a Q
  - Or Q Singleton, or Qx

---

## Dummy Points
- A = 4, K = 3, Q = 2, J = 1
- **1** for doubleton
- **2** for singleton
- **2 ½** for void

If 6 ½, go half the time.

---

## Normal Responses to Overcall System
- **2D** to **2C** then pass or bid 2 Major
- Best Major to **2D** or bid 6-card minor if 22(63)
- Pass or bid 6-card suit if singleton in partner’s 2 of Major
